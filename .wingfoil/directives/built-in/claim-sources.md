# Sources of claims

A claim names what establishes it.

- A statement of fact in an element, a report or a message names its source: the element, the
  file, the command, the commit or the person it comes from.
- A claim not checked is said to be unchecked.
- A pack or a project may tighten this rule with a directive of its own, for example asking for the
  exact command that establishes a claim about code.
