## Context

The script runner (dl-023, dl-024) launches one workflow step at a time and sees only the step's
actions and the tasks' `depends_on`. Its pilot in `sprint-002` (2026-11-21) found four things this
repository's governance holds only as directive or plan text:

- At `backlog-item.start` of `task-091` the agent moved the task but not `bug-053`: the prompt lists
  the step's actions, and `start` has only `element.set_state(in-progress)`. The facilitator moved
  the bug by hand.
- v0.3.1's waves were plan text: nine `depends_on` amends by the approver encoded them (N-003).
- The wave gate (`definition-of-done`) and the phase branch (`release-planning` › *Review*) are no
  step: the runner would have integrated `task-091` into `main` until the approver switched the line.
- Smaller: rule 13 reads as if `--doctor` passed right after the switch; `launch.manual` must stay
  empty (runner review L-5); the `branch-<line>` notice is committed on the wrong branch.

WingFoil 0.3.2's built-in `bug.sync_state(for_each: task.bug)` names a task's bugs and moves each to
the task's state: a probe shows it at `start` (`planned → in-progress`) and `review` (`in-progress →
in-review`), satisfied by `memory submit`. Two approver phases added to `sprint.yaml`, probed the
same way, reopen `sprint-001` and stop the running `sprint-002`.

## Decision

1. **Deviation R-10 of `backlog-item`:** `bug.sync_state(for_each: task.bug)` at `start` and at
   `review`, done with `memory submit <bug>` for each bug `workflow next` names. Not at `done`: the
   bug's `in-review → resolved` (with the task's approve) and `resolved → closed` (after its close
   note) stay the approver's, by `gate.mjs --element`.
2. **The waves are `depends_on`,** written by the product-owner at *build-backlog* in each task's
   draft, before its `memory submit`: every task of wave N depends on the gate task of wave N−1, and
   the tasks of wave 1 on the phase-branch task. The plan's waves list the same order.
3. **A gate task per wave:** "Wave N gate — vX.Y.Z", with `depends_on` every task of the wave and the
   wave gate's checks as its Acceptance Criteria (`gates.yml`'s `wave` scope on the phase branch's
   tip, `test:smoke`, `test:timing`, `npm audit --omit=dev`, the wave record, the merge into `main`).
   The runner launches it as any backlog item; what its agent may not do (a push, `gh`, the
   machine-free slot) it asks for with `RESULT: blocked decision`, and the approver, or a session
   outside the runner, does it. The last wave's gate also moves the line back to `main`.
4. **A phase-branch task:** "Phase branch — vX.Y.Z", the first of the Sprint Backlog: the phase branch
   from `main` with its `PHASE_BRANCH` commit, pushed, and the line switched (rule 13), asked as a
   decision and verified by the agent. No phase is added to `sprint.yaml`.
5. **`wingfoil-cli`:** rule 10 keeps `launch.manual` empty until the runner's L-5 is fixed; rule 13
   says `--doctor` fails until the first launch after a switch; rule 14 says the `branch-<line>`
   notice is read from the runner's log; W-55 and note F-043 record the `sync` commit no verb writes.
6. **Where:** on `main` and on `design/dev-loop_v0.3.1`. v0.3.1 keeps its approved `depends_on`; its
   wave gates stay with the facilitator outside the runner; decisions 2–4 start at v0.3.2's planning.

## Rationale

- An action in the step is what the runner shows the agent; a directive line alone was not followed.
- `sync_state` is WingFoil's own built-in, so R-10 adds no binding and no check; `done` would target
  `closed` and skip two approver gates.
- `depends_on` is the one ordering the runner honours: tasks carry both the waves and the gates, with
  no change to `sprint.yaml` and no reopened Sprint.
- Writing them in the draft keeps them the product-owner's work, not the approver's amends.
