## Rationale

- Action 1: the release passed every automated gate while two of its exit criteria had no evidence; a
  named check per criterion makes that visible before the approval, not at the Sprint Review. The
  prototype has not followed the product: of the 95 capabilities counted in it that shipped in `0.3.0`,
  25 changed by a recorded decision (`retrospective-rel-v0.3-plan`, RT3-4), and `ux-reference.md`
  records the decided deviations.
- Action 2: 7 of the 15 FAIL rounds were parser cost or correctness, and each fix bounded only the
  reported path (task-075, task-078). A probe at design and a growth assertion cover the class.
- Action 3: 8 runtime findings surfaced only at `pre-release-checks` and forced bug-050 and task-083;
  a wave gate finds them while a task can still absorb them.
- Action 4: 9 of the 19 planned tasks were split at design, and the design estimates sum to about 30
  active days against the 10 of development (13 with release validation, RP3-7) while the stage closed
  in the Sprint. Working time was asked for by
  dl-006, dl-009 and RP3-12 and not recorded: the wave gate gives the figure a fixed place. Trade-off:
  with agents working in parallel the active day stays a coarse measure (A3).
- Action 5: dl-009's actions 3 and 10 were deferred to v0.4 and are named in no v0.4 record.
- Action 6: the approver's choice, as WingFoil ran its own releases; a series avoids two lines of
  development on one repository.
