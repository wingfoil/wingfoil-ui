**Design (retrospective).**

- Cause (AC-1): the test, not the product. The lost tab's probe (`GET /api/server`) is sent after
  the same-origin exchange's response (BroadcastChannel message). The old test listened to the probe
  on `page` and to `same 204` on a second page, and asserted their relative index in one array
  (`order.indexOf('same 204')` = 1, `probe` after it). Playwright delivers the events of two pages
  through two channels and orders nothing across them, so the probe could be recorded first. The
  product's request order is unchanged, so AC-3's product branch does not apply: no product change,
  no regression test at unit/server level, no spec-004 Process Note, no CHANGELOG line.
- Fix: no assertion compares an event of one page with an event of another. The probes are counted
  (`probes`) at points the test orders by awaiting: `0` after the foreign-origin exchange and its
  tab's render, `>= 1` after the recovery (the page reloaded). No retry added; the checks are not
  loosened in kind (the foreign exchange still must not trigger a probe, the recovery still must).
- AC classification: AC-1 and AC-2 are test-level (e2e + CI workflow); AC-3 is *not applicable* (cause
  in the test). Scenario → test: X-02.3 (`local-security.feature`) → `test/e2e/session-recovery.spec.ts`
  › "X-02.3 — an exchange on another port leaves the page lost"; the `repeat` input of

  `test/package/gates-workflow.test.ts`.
